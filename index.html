How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, 2–6 months is a good starting point. For portraits and smaller shoots, contact us and we'll check availability.

Yes. We can travel across India for weddings, events and commercial assignments. Travel costs are discussed before booking.

How do I receive my photographs?

Your final edited photographs are delivered through a private online gallery so you can view, download and share them easily.

Absolutely. Tell us what you need and we'll create a package around your event, duration and deliverables.
